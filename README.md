  - 🌎 **Americas (20 Regions)**: USA, Canada, Mexico, Brazil, Argentina, Colombia, Chile, Peru, Ecuador, Uruguay, Paraguay, Bolivia, Costa Rica, Panama, Dominican Republic, Puerto Rico, Guatemala, Jamaica, Trinidad & Tobago, Latin America & Caribbean.
  - 🌏 **Asia & Pacific (23 Regions)**: Japan, China, Hong Kong, Taiwan, South Korea, Singapore, Australia, New Zealand, India, Nepal, Sri Lanka, Philippines, Thailand, Vietnam, Malaysia, Indonesia, Pakistan & Bangladesh, Mongolia, Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, Cambodia, Myanmar, Fiji & Pacific Islands.
  - 🏜️ **Middle East & Africa (20 Regions)**: Iraq (Mesopotamia), Saudi Arabia, UAE, Jordan, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman, Turkey, Iran, Israel, Egypt, Lebanon, Morocco, Tunisia, Ghana, Uganda, Kenya, Ethiopia, South Africa, Nigeria.
- **Dual-Row Segmented Navigation**:
  - **Row 1 (Continents)**: Modern dark glassmorphic pill tabs with ambient glowing borders, active state highlights, and real-time country counts.
  - **Row 2 (Countries & Search)**: Instant live search filter, fast dropdown picker with dynamic region headers, and horizontally scrollable flag pills.
- **Smooth Chevron Controls & Dark Scrolling**:
  - Added modern left/right chevron buttons (`<` and `>`) for effortless horizontal navigation.
  - Hidden native scrollbars (`no-scrollbar`) with smooth wheel-scroll and touch-swipe support across all viewports.
- **1-Tap Direct Action**:
  - Direct telephone dial links (`tel:...`) with glowing call buttons.

- **Smart Automatic Detection**:
  - Zero-latency browser timezone and locale detection (`detectUserCountryCode`) automatically sets the visitor's continent and country on first visit.
