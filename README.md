## 🌟 Major Milestone: 100% Global Coverage via ISO 3166-1 Standard (252 Regions & Territories) 🌍

`why-you-matter.org` now features one of the most comprehensive, privacy-first mental health and crisis directory engines on the web, fully compliant with the **international ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 standard**. 

We ensure that **no human being on Earth is left without a lifeline**—covering all 193 UN member states, 2 UN permanent observer states (Vatican & Palestine), plus all autonomous territories, overseas departments, and isolated island communities (249 official ISO 3166-1 codes / 252 total regional entities).

### ⚡ Architectural & UX Innovations:
- **Dual-Row Segmented Engine**:
  - **Row 1 (Continents)**: Modern dark glassmorphic pill tabs with ambient glowing borders, active state highlights, and real-time live country counters (`All 252`, `Europe 58`, `Americas 57`, `Asia & Pacific 60`, `Middle East & Africa 77`).
  - **Row 2 (Countries & Search)**: Instant sub-millisecond search filter, fast dropdown picker with dynamic region headers (`Countries in Americas`, `Countries in Europe`, etc.), and horizontally scrollable flag pills.
- **Smooth Chevron Controls & Dark Scrolling**:
  - Sleek `<` and `>` chevron controls for touchless, effortless horizontal browsing.
  - Universal dark scrollbar normalization (`no-scrollbar` + `glow-scrollbar`) preventing bright OS scrollbars from interrupting the calming atmosphere.
- **1-Tap Direct Action**:
  - Direct telephone dial links (`tel:...`) with glowing call buttons.
  - Direct SMS text lines (`sms:...`) with sky-blue styling.
  - Verified badges (`24/7`, `Free`, `Chat`) and local general emergency numbers (🚨 `112`, `911`, `999`, `119`, `115`, `110`, etc.).
- **Smart Automatic Detection**:
  - Zero-latency client-side browser timezone and locale detection (`detectUserCountryCode`) automatically sets the visitor's continent and country on first visit without tracking or external IP lookups.
